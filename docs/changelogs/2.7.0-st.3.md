# ChatPlus Staff Tools 2.7.0-st.3

Staff features. Everything from st.2 is included.

## Player list
- A searchable list of everyone you've seen online on the server, online players first, then the most recently seen.
- Click a name for the same menu as right-clicking it in chat, so you can act on players who aren't in chat or are offline.
- Open it with `/mt players` or the new **P** button on the chat bar (only on servers in your profile).
- Saved every 5 minutes and when you leave, in `config/chatplus/moderation/players/<server>.json`: username, UUID, first seen and last seen. Nothing else, and only on your PC.

## Before punishing
- Warn and Temp-ban show the player's last 3 messages from chat.
- A warning when someone with a look-alike name is online, such as `Steve_`, `5teve` or `Stevee` for `Steve`.
- An "(offline)" tag when the player isn't online.

## Location and record on Warn, Temp-ban and Note
- Shows where you are (coordinates, dimension, biome), the date and time, and the player's record from this session's lookup.
- **Insert location** and **Insert time** add them to the reason.
- **Chunk owner** sends the profile's `chunk_owner` command when clicked and shows the server's reply on the screen. New `"parser": "lines"` for commands whose reply is only shown.
- A command's optional `reply` patterns pick out its reply lines, so other server messages are left out.
- When the chunk owner is a team, the button becomes **Team members** and asks the server for the team's owner and members (profile `team_info`, with the `{team}` and `{teamId}` placeholders).
- A server message with several lines is matched line by line.

## Fixed
- ChatPlus's chat shortcuts (peek chat, speech-to-text record and quick send, hide chat, speak mode) no longer fire while you type in a text box on another screen, such as the warn reason or a search box.

## Missing-slash guard
- A chat message that looks like a command without its `/` is held back with a note, and your text stays in the box. Press Enter again within 10 seconds to send it as chat.
- It holds back a server command followed by one of its sub-commands (such as `staff ban ...`), and any first word in the profile's new `slashGuard` list. Ordinary sentences go through.
- Tab auto-prefixes are respected, so a tab that adds `/msg Steve ` still sends normally.
- On by default. Turn it off in ChatPlus settings, Moderation, "Hold Back Commands Without /".
